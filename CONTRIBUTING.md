# Contributing

You do not need to agree with the project's current proposals to contribute.

Useful contributions identify factual errors, provide stronger sources, challenge assumptions, expose unintended consequences, propose alternatives, test mechanisms, or demonstrate that a proposal should be abandoned.

## Principles versus mechanisms

The project distinguishes between **foundational principles** and **institutional mechanisms**. Principles define the intended direction. Mechanisms are provisional means of advancing those principles and may be revised or discarded.

## Political neutrality

This is an institutional-design inquiry, not an instrument for promoting a particular political party, candidate, ideology, or electoral outcome.

## Good-faith disagreement

Criticism is encouraged. Personal attacks, harassment, threats, doxxing, and attempts to silence disagreement are not.

**The standard is reasoned scrutiny.**
