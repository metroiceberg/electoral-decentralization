# ADR-0001: Project Philosophy

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-22

## Context

The project needs a stable foundation while allowing its institutional mechanisms to evolve.

## Decision

The project distinguishes between:

- **Foundational principles**, which define the intended direction; and
- **Institutional mechanisms**, which are provisional hypotheses.

The ultimate objective is to investigate **decentralization of electoral power** while preserving meaningful popular sovereignty and governmental functionality.

Mechanisms may be revised, replaced, or abandoned when evidence, criticism, experimentation, or reason demonstrates that another mechanism better serves the foundational principles.

## Guiding statement

> **The principles are the foundation. The mechanisms are hypotheses.**
