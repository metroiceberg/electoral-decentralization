# Changelog

## 2026-09-22

### Foundational repository created

Initial public foundation established:

- project purpose;
- popular sovereignty;
- decentralization of electoral power as the ultimate objective;
- separation of qualification, endorsement, and election;
- political parties as participants rather than necessary gatekeepers;
- institutional capture as a primary design risk; and
- public scrutiny and revision as permanent features.

All specific institutional mechanisms remain provisional.
